## Rulings taken in this plan (defaults; tell KC, none blocks)
1. **Front-desk plan change uses `members.changePlan` (gated), not a `PlanChangeRequest` row.** The existing gate already turns a non-management change into a `ChangeRequest` that management approves in Approvals (round 5 rule), shows the pending banner with Withdraw on the Plan tab, and replays the change on approval; `planRequests.test.ts:47` already says "staff use members.changePlan". `planChange.request` stays family-only. Cost if wrong: the request would not show in the family-request Note on the Plan tab or as "asked: plan" on Renewals — a small follow-up.
2. **Lobby payments are confirmed by finance (Payments page) or management (Approvals → Payments).** A waiting payment counts toward the invoice balance for staff (so nobody records it twice and the stop job does not fire), but families, the Month report "Collected", Payments tiles, Xero and refunds see it only once confirmed. Rejected = soft-deleted, invoice open again.
3. **Lead funnel is a cohort:** for a period, the leads opened in it and how far each got (visit, trial, joined, lost), so the funnel never exceeds 100%. The Month report pipeline, the Enquiries History view and the hero conversion all use the same `leadFunnel`.
4. **Hero sparklines** use the six whole months ending with the last whole month on or before the period's end (This month on 22 Oct → **Apr–Sep**; Last month → **Apr–Sep** too; a custom period ending 31 Aug → Mar–Aug); the tile's value and delta are the selected period's own, as in the groups.
5. **Leave days** count open club days only (`dayStatus(s, d).open`); a request stays inside one calendar year; management may decide its own request (one manager in the club). **Controller ruling (22 Oct):** People gets the new **Leave** tab in Attendance's place in the tab order, and the Attendance tab stays after it renamed **Hours** (clock in/out and the month's worked rows keep working). The spec said replace, but removing it would cost management a working feature; cost if wrong: one extra tab.
6. **Lobby surveys:** `survey.send` gains an optional `familyId` — with it, a live family survey is sent to one more contact (lobby or management); without it, the existing draft send (management only).

## Review Focus
1. A lobby user calling `payment.record` with `dokuVa`/`other`, without `invoiceId`, or above the balance is refused server-side; a finance user calling `approval.approve` with any type but `payments` is refused (Task 3).
2. A waiting payment never reaches the family projection, the payer's notification, Xero, refunds or Month report "Collected" before confirmation; a rejected one re-opens the invoice (Task 3).
3. A lobby `members.changePlan` with an effective date other than the 1st of next month is refused at submission; the approval replay notifies the family once (Task 3).
4. A join that management rejects (or the desk withdraws) leaves an `undo` move in the lead's history and the lead is not counted as joined (Task 2).
5. A leave request over the balance, overlapping another, in the past or across a year is refused; approval writes one `staffTime` `leave` row per open day and cancellation removes them (Task 6).
6. Seed: no RNG draw added; small-seed counts unchanged (`e2e/enquiries.spec.ts:73` `{ new: 1, visit: 2, trial: 1, joined: 0, lost: 1 }`); no seeded past lead has a `next` (`seed/anchors.test.ts:54`).
