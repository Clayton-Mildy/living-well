# Round 20 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make field trip days work end to end:
- check-in puts a member on the trip;
- trips count as visit days;
- the desk and nurse get a banner;
- meals are optional;
- the family sees "On the field trip";
- the camera is refused on closed days.

**Architecture:** Shared rules and actions come first (trip membership, visit days, optional menu, duties). The staff and family screens consume them; the seed, e2e and screenshots come last. The tasks run **one after another** in the main checkout, because the machine is CPU-constrained.

**Tech Stack:** pnpm monorepo, TypeScript, React 18, Hono + Postgres (5434), Vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-round20-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **Code layout:**
  - shared immer actions live in `packages/shared/src/actions` (`defineAction`);
  - pure rules live in `packages/shared/src/rules`;
  - EN and ID strings live in i18n, with ID typed `Same<typeof EN.x>`.
- **UI:** dense, phone-first, ink/grey/white, no pastel tints, no filler text, and tablet must work.
- **CPU (the laptop crashed under load):**
  - vitest runs with `--maxWorkers=2`;
  - focused files while iterating, and the full suite once before each commit;
  - one e2e group;
  - never two test runs at once;
  - after e2e, drop the `citrapremier_e2e_<name><n>` databases and the `.e2e-<name>` folders.
- **Port:** 8788 for the API, never 8787.
- **Seed:** RNG-neutral; `CP_SEED_SMALL=1` keeps 5 members; state under 7,500,000 characters.
- **Trip day:** a date where `eventsOn(s, date)` has `kind === 'outing'`. Several on one day: use the first by `from`, then by `id`.
- **Trip charge:** id `trip-<eventId>-<memberId>`, label `inv.line.fieldTrip`, one per trip (not per day). An invoiced charge is never removed (`err.charged` stays for `setWent`).
- **Banner copy:** "Field trip today" / "Karyawisata hari ini", then "{title} · {from}–{to}" and "{n} going" / "{n} ikut".
- **Kitchen copy:** "Field trip · menu optional" / "Karyawisata · menu opsional", and "Add what they'll eat" / "Tambah menu hari itu".
- **Family copy:**
  - hero: "On the field trip · {title}" / "Ikut karyawisata · {title}";
  - return row: "Back at the club · going home" / "Kembali ke klub · pulang".
- **Commit trailer:** `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Rulings (made while planning)

- **R1:** check-in and undo update the trip through one helper, `setTripMember(d, ev, memberId, on, ctx)`, in `actions/schedule.ts`. `setWent` is refactored to use it. The behaviour stays the same: fee 0 means no charge, and an invoiced charge blocks removal.
- **R2:** `undoCheckIn` on a trip day removes the member from `wentIds` only when the trip charge is un-invoiced. If the charge is invoiced, the undo still succeeds and the member stays on the trip. In that case `ctx.result.tripKept = { invoiceId, amount }`, and Arrivals shows a toast: "Check-in undone. {name} stays on the trip: the {amount} fee is on invoice {invoiceId}. Finance can remove it." / "Check-in dibatalkan. {name} tetap ikut karyawisata: biaya {amount} sudah di tagihan {invoiceId}. Bagian keuangan dapat menghapusnya." That toast belongs to Task 3, and the `tripKept` result belongs to Task 1.
- **R3:** `visitDaysOf(s, memberId, from, to): ISODate[]` returns the sorted unique days with a check-in, plus the trip days where the member is in `wentIds`. Few visits and the Month report use it. Flex quota and billing don't.
- **R4:** "a menu was added" means a date override exists for that date (the existing per-date menu change). `menuOn` on a trip day returns that override or `null`, never the weekly template.

## Review Focus

1. **A member checked in twice on a trip day** (check-in, undo, check-in again) ends up on the trip once with one charge. Pinned in Task 1.
2. **A multi-day trip:** checking in on day 1 and again on day 2 gives one charge, not two. Pinned in Task 1.
3. **A trip with no fee** (fee 0): check-in adds the member to `wentIds` and creates no charge. Pinned in Task 1.
4. **A trip day with a kitchen menu set:** lunch duties, the lunch round and allergen alerts all run. Without a menu, none of them run. Pinned in Task 2.
5. **A parent ticked in Who went without checking in** (they joined the bus elsewhere): the family still sees "On the field trip", and the day counts for few visits. Pinned in Tasks 1 and 4.
