## Rulings taken in this plan (defaults; tell KC, none blocks)
1. **"Applies from the 1st" keeps today's mechanism.** `applyChange` (`S/actions/renewals.ts:60`) already writes effective-dated entries (a plan entry from `monthStart`, leave months, `applyEnding` with the last day). Without approval it simply runs at once. No new job is needed. "Undo until the 1st" uses a new `followUp.undo`, which reverses those entries while the month has not started.
2. **The front desk's plan change loses its gate.** `members.changePlan` drops `review: { policy: 'gate' }` for the `plan` section. The lobby keeps the first-of-next-month restriction (`S/actions/members.ts:438`). `planChange.apply` / `planChange.decline` for family requests (finance/mgmt) are unchanged.
3. **Door mood becomes optional in the UI.** The server already treats it as optional (`optFace`). `MatchedPanel` and `CheckoutDialog` enable Confirm without a face. `CheckoutDialog.test.tsx:34` changes on purpose.
4. **Face meanings are added under the existing labels.** "Great / Good / Okay / Low / Upset" stay (e2e and tests read them). The spec's meanings become one hint line per face (`mood.hint.5..1`): "Chatting and laughing", "Engaged and content", "Calm, quiet but fine", "Withdrawn or tired", "Distressed, agitated or in pain". In ID: "Mengobrol dan tertawa", "Terlibat dan senang", "Tenang, diam tapi baik", "Menarik diri atau lelah", "Gelisah, cemas atau kesakitan".
5. **The daily log's mood word goes away for staff and family.** `DailyLog.mood` stays in the type for old data. Every display that read it uses `dailyMood` instead. The activity "Mood & notes" round becomes the **Morning** round (morning face + notes). In `ActivityToday`, a member counts as "logged" once they have a morning mark or a saved note.
6. **Pending programme day-changes live beside the live day, not in it.** `ScheduleDay.pending?: { slots, extra?, note?, by, at }` holds the activity team's edits and `ScheduleDay.returned?: { note, by, at }` holds Ega's note. Every rule (`sessionsOn`, `dayInfo`, the family projection) keeps reading the live fields, so no consumer needs an "approved only" flag. Approve merges `pending` into the live fields; return clears it and stores `returned`. Weekly versions use the existing `status: 'pending' | 'rejected'` and `rejectNote` (publishedVersions already ignores non-published ones).
7. **The activity team gets the programme builder.** Today every schedule action is `mgmtOnly` (`S/actions/schedule.ts:13`) and `canBuild = role === 'mgmt'` (`W/features/calendar/Calendar.tsx:37`). Both widen to activity, and activity output goes to approval. Calendar-event, activity and room upserts stay management-only.
8. **The Prepare button works for activity too.** `celebration.request` widens its `can` from `lobbyOrMgmt` to lobby, activity and mgmt. An activity request is `requested`, like the lobby's. The request form is lifted out of `Celebrations.tsx` into a reusable sheet that accepts a prefill.
9. **Weekend open days are decided in `core.ts`, without the kitchen rules.** `programmedWeekend(s, date)` reads `s.scheduleVersions` (published) and `s.scheduleDays[date]` (live) directly, which avoids an import cycle with `rules/kitchen.ts`. A holiday or closure on a weekend still closes it.
10. **The dementia invoice line gets its own kind.** `InvoiceLine.kind` gains `'dementia'` (label `inv.line.dementia`, id `dementia-${period}`). It is added in `runLinesFor` after the plan line, and skipped in a leave month.

## Review Focus
1. **Undoing a recorded change.** A stop, a leave or a plan switch recorded for next month and then undone before the 1st leaves no plan entry, leave month or ending behind. An undo after the 1st is refused (`renewals.err.started`). Owned by Task 1.
2. **Paid invoices drop off Unpaid.** An invoice that becomes fully paid (including a waiting front-desk payment) leaves the list at once. A voided invoice never shows. A `promised` date that has passed puts the row back at the top. Owned by Task 2.
3. **Old mood words.** A day with `DailyLog.mood` and no face marks shows no mood (not "Okay"). `dailyMood` never averages marks from other days or other members. Owned by Task 6.
4. **Weekend days and holidays.** A weekend with only a pending programme, or only a returned one, stays closed. A weekend holiday with an approved programme stays closed. Flex counting includes a weekend visit. Owned by Task 11.
5. **Reverting to an empty day.** An activity edit that returns a day to its published state (no difference) clears `pending` instead of sending an empty approval. Ega approving a version whose `effectiveFrom` has passed publishes it from today. Owned by Task 10.
