# Round 16 Phase 1 (day-to-day) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Round 16 points 1–7:
- Flex and Gold chips beside Active;
- a configurable notice period;
- simulated emails (contract, invoices);
- switching the billing contact;
- birthday charges on their own invoice;
- pick-up and field trip fees;
- proof of payment.

**Architecture:**
- Pure rules in `packages/shared/src/rules`, immer actions in `actions`, as in rounds 12–15.
- One new collection, `emails`: a simulated outbox. Its rows are the record; nothing leaves the server.
- New fees ride on what exists:
  - the pick-up fee is an invoice line built in `runLinesFor`, like the dementia fee;
  - a field trip charge is a `PendingCharge`, already billed by `runLinesFor`;
  - a birthday invoice is an `Invoice` of kind `'manual'`, which the VA flow (`payment.simulateVa`) already pays together with other open invoices.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, immer actions, zod (some actions), React 18, vitest, Playwright; pnpm monorepo.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-round16-design.md` §Phase 1 (points 1–7), Conventions and Testing. KC approved the spec on 30 Oct.

## Global Constraints

- Shared immer actions in `packages/shared/src/actions`, pure rules in `rules`. Role checks are server-side through `can`.
- Dense phone-first UI, 44 px targets, ink, grey and white with the warm staff skin, no pastel tints. Verify at phone, tablet and desktop.
- Every string in EN and ID (`packages/shared/src/i18n/en`, `id`).
- Family edits go through approvals, except the billing contact switch (point 6).
- Seed RNG-neutral, `CP_SEED_SMALL=1` working, club state under 7,500,000 characters at the real date and at 2026-12-30.
- Minimal tests: typecheck, unit tests for new rules and actions, one e2e pass for the phase.
- Notice: "the deadline for a change starting in month M is `1st of M − noticeDays` (December → 17 November)", default 14 days.
- Upgrades need no notice (until the day before the 1st). Downgrade, stop and leave need it.
- Proof of payment: "bank transfer or cash requires a photo … DOKU VA payments and `other` don't need one."

## Rulings made while writing this plan

- **R1 Notice applies to:** `followUp.record`, `planChange.request` (family), `members.changePlan`, `membership.requestLeave` and `leaveCheck`. It does **not** apply to `members.end`. That is management ending a membership for a reason (passed away, health, club decision), not the family's request.
- **R2 Leave deadline moves by one day.** Today it is 14 days before the end of the previous month (16 Nov for December). It becomes `noticeDeadline(s, month)`: 17 Nov for December, as KC chose ("14 days before the 1st").
- **R3 Downgrade test:** a move to a plan whose monthly price on the 1st of M is lower than or equal to the current plan's price is a downgrade (`switch` included). A higher price is an upgrade.
- **R4 Family plan request month:** `planChange.request` sets `from` to the 1st of the earliest allowed month for that change (`earliestMonth`), not always next month.
- **R5 Default fees:** pick-up Rp 600.000 a month; field trip Rp 250.000 a trip. They are added to `FEE_DEFAULTS` and the seed's price list.
- **R6 Birthday invoice:** numbered `CEL-YYMM-NNN` (YYMM from the issue date, NNN counts that month's celebration invoices). Due in 7 days. One line per uninvoiced charge, of kind `'celebration'`.
- **R7 Contract email trigger:** a helper `maybeEmailContract(d, memberId, ctx)` is called after `signing.stamp`, `signing.physical`, `activateMember` (members.create) and `finishJoin` (enquiry.convert). It sends once per signing: it skips when an `emails` row of kind `contract` with `ref.id === <memberId>:<last signing step at>` exists.
- **R8 Billing switch:** keep the action name `family.setPrimary`.
  - Drop its review gate.
  - Widen `can` to the member's current primary family contact.
  - Add: unpaid invoices move to the new contact, and each moved invoice is emailed.
- **R9 New collection `emails`:** follow the round 14 `unpaidFollowUps` pattern (`git log -S unpaidFollowUps --oneline` shows the commit):
  - add it to `COLLECTIONS` and `ClubState`;
  - add the named export in `apps/server/src/db/schema.ts`;
  - add the empty `{}` wherever a fresh state is built;
  - add the family projection, where families see only their own rows.

## Review Focus

1. **A change asked on the deadline day:** asked on 17 Nov for December, it is allowed. On 18 Nov, the earliest month is January. *Test: Task 2.*
2. **An upgrade after the deadline:** asked on 25 Nov for December, it is still allowed. *Test: Task 2.*
3. **Billing switch with a part-paid invoice:** the invoice moves with its balance, and the payment history stays on the invoice. *Test: Task 5.*
4. **Pick-up during leave or after a stop:** no pick-up line in a leave month, nor in a month after the last day. *Test: Task 7.*
5. **Unticking a field trip:** the charge is removed if it is not yet invoiced. Once it is on an invoice, unticking is refused (`err.charged`). *Test: Task 8.*
