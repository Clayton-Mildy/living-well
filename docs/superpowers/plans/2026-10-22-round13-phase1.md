**Goal:** Land the six quick wins KC wants before the owner review: clearer Members page section names, a readable Approvals bottom bar, a free-text "Other" title on both registration paths, check-in/check-out counts in the lobby's duties, and guest hosts visible to the front desk.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-round13-lobby-owner-design.md` (phase 1 sections 1–5)

- Every new string in `packages/shared/src/i18n/en/*` and `id/*` (`Same<typeof EN>`).
- Tests: `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test <file>` for touched rules/actions/components; no matrices.
- Commit per task with trailer `Co-Authored-By: Claude Fable 5.1 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- Round 12 e2e specs must keep passing: run `scripts/e2e-all.sh 1 "phone" "lobby|members|enquiries|round12"` once at the end (Task 5).

## Review Focus
1. A custom title longer than 20 characters or empty: refused with `members.err.titleRequired` / `err.invalid`, never stored (Task 3).
2. Initials for a member with a custom title ("Tante Lily Tan") must not take the title's first letter (Task 3).
3. The check-in duty on a closed day or before opening: status `none`, no badge, no reminder (Task 4).
4. A guest session cancelled after booking: the rail block no longer lists it and no stale notification is created (Task 5).
5. The Approvals toolbar with zero selected rows: Approve/Reject disabled and visibly so on the grey background (Task 2).
