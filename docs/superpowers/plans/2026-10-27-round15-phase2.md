# Round 15 Phase 2 (bigger features) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The five bigger round 15 features:
- reviewing the programme inside the Calendar;
- a catalogue of custom membership plans used everywhere;
- signing at registration;
- the directory grouped by editable categories;
- dish photos.

**Architecture:**
- Plan definitions and directory categories live in `ClubSettings` (no new tables). Custom plans' monthly prices live in the existing effective-dated `PriceVersion` (a `plans` map), so a price changes "from a date" exactly like Flex and Gold.
- The `Plan` type widens to Flex, Gold or a catalogue id. Every hard-coded Flex/Gold check moves to a few shared helpers: `planDef`, `isDaysPlan`, `planQuota`, `planPrice`, `planLabel`.
- Programme review reuses the round 14 approval actions inside the Calendar UI.
- Signing at registration reuses the round 14 phase D signing model.
- Dishes gain a photo field.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, immer actions, React 18, vitest, Playwright; pnpm monorepo.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-round15-design.md` §Phase 2 (points 13–17) and its Conventions and Testing sections. KC confirmed the spec assumptions on 27 Oct, including signed-in-person counting as signed and directory categories being separate from spending categories.

**Depends on:** round 15 phase 1 (`activePlans(s)` from its Task 8 is extended here).

## Global Constraints

- The same conventions as phase 1:
  - shared actions and rules, role checks through `can`;
  - dense phone-first UI, 44 px targets, no pastel tints;
  - EN and ID strings;
  - family edits go through approvals;
  - seed RNG-neutral, `CP_SEED_SMALL=1`, state under 7,500,000 characters at the real date and 2026-12-30;
  - minimal tests plus one e2e pass.
- **Plan types:** "Days per month with a quota (1–23), or Unlimited; a monthly price that changes from a chosen date; an Active switch; a name in EN and ID; Quick add 'Start from Flex' / 'Start from Gold'." Flex and Gold are the catalogue's first two plans and keep their prices in the price list. A retired plan leaves the pickers, and its members keep it.
- **Signing:** a registration signature makes the membership form **signed in person**, which then goes to countersign and e-Meterai exactly like an online signature (round 14 phase D model: `signing.status 'signed'`, `signerId`, the `form` snapshot, the `signed` step).
- **Directory:** "Contacts are grouped by category, with a call button on each contact. The category list is editable by the front desk, finance and management (add, rename, retire)." Suppliers keep their spending category.
- **Dish photos:** "show in the menu plan, the staff calendar's day view, the family app's tomorrow timeline and the family calendar; on the day, once F&B uploads a lunch photo, that photo replaces the dish photo for that day."

## Rulings made while writing this plan

- **R1 Plan ids:** `type Plan = 'flex' | 'gold' | \`plan_${string}\``. Catalogue entries are `ClubSettings.plans: PlanDef[]`. Flex and Gold are virtual entries built from settings and the price list, not stored.
- **R2 Custom plan prices:** they live in `PriceVersion.plans?: Record<string, number>`, set through the existing `prices.set`. A custom plan with no price in force bills Rp 0 and shows "No price" in Plans and pricing.
- **R3 Extra days:** extra days on any days plan are charged at the club's `price.extra`. Flex's quota stays `settings.flexQuota`.
- **R4 Empty week in the seed:** a published empty version effective Monday of the week containing T+14. Then a version effective the following Monday restores the normal days, so the programme comes back after one week.
- **R5 Registration signature:**
  - The signature is optional at the last step.
  - With one, `members.create` / `enquiry.convert` creates the signing as `signed`, with steps `draft`, `sent` and `signed`, all at the creation time. The `draft` and `sent` steps are by the staff user; the `signed` step is by the billing contact, who is the signer.
  - Without one, nothing changes.
- **R6 Dish photo on the day:** the photo shown on a date is that date's lunch photo when one exists, else the dish photo. One shared rule decides, `lunchPictureOn(s, date)`.

## Review Focus

1. **Retired plans:** a member on a retired custom plan still bills and still shows the plan's name everywhere. Pickers no longer offer it. *Tests: Task 1 and Task 3.*
2. **Price changes mid-period:** a custom plan's price changed for next month bills the old price this month and the new price from the 1st. *Test: Task 1.*
3. **Days plan quota:** a custom days plan with a quota of 8 makes the 9th visit an extra day. The Unlimited type never makes extra days. *Test: Task 1.*
4. **A proposal that changes in the background:** a proposal approved from the Calendar after it was re-sent or already decided reports a conflict, the same as Approvals. *Test: Task 4.*
5. **Retired directory categories:** contacts in a retired category still show, under "Other". The category disappears from the pickers. *Test: Task 6.*
