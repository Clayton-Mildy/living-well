# Round 21 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Monthly opt-in field trip fee with a missed-month credit, trip venues as Contacts (with images), pick-up priced by distance, an Insights "Where from" map, and 1–5 star contact ratings.

**Architecture:** Shared immer actions and pure rules in `packages/shared` hold every rule (billing, distance, grouping, ratings, the family copy). The Hono server adds one geocoding proxy with a fake provider for tests. The web adds one `LocationPicker` (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap) used by members, enquiries, contacts and Settings, plus the new Insights tab.

**Tech Stack:** pnpm monorepo, TypeScript, React 18 + Vite, Hono, Drizzle/Postgres, vitest, Playwright. New dependency: `leaflet@1.9.4` and `@types/leaflet@1.9.12` (web only).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-round21-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Every new label in EN and ID (`packages/shared/src/i18n/en/*` and `id/*`; the `Same<typeof EN>` type enforces the pair).
- Phone first, dense rows, filters as dropdowns (`Select`); no pastel tints (grey/white/ink, gold only as the brand gold `#CFA550`).
- Pick-up: `price = ceil(km) × perKm`, free when `km <= freeKm`; defaults `perKm = 10_000`, `freeKm = 2`; km is straight-line to 0.1.
- Field trip monthly fee = the price list's `fieldTrip` (default Rp 250,000); opt-in per member; flat, not prorated, none in a leave month.
- Venue: trading name (`name`), address, contact person and phone required; images optional, at most 10.
- Ratings: integer stars 1–5, note at most 200 characters; family never get ratings, venue images, contact person or location of a contact.
- Geo search runs on Enter or the Search button only (no type-ahead); the proxy allows at most 1 Nominatim request a second and caches for 24 h; `CP_GEO_FAKE=1` answers from a fixed list.
- Low CPU: one test runner at a time, `pnpm vitest run <files> --maxWorkers=2`; full suites once before the final commit; one e2e group; drop `citrapremier_e2e_*` databases and `.e2e-*` folders after.
- Commit messages end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- Merge note: the `bulk-import` branch has uncommitted work adding `'other'` to `MEMBER_FILTERS`; Task 10 adds `'trips'`. Expect a small conflict there when both land.

## Review Focus

1. **The free-distance edge.** 2.0 km is free, but 2.05 km rounds up and is billed as 3 km (Rp 30,000). Pinned in Task 1.
2. **Switched on after the month's invoice.** A member switched on for field trips after that month's invoice exists gets no trip line that month, and no credit for it next month. Pinned in Task 10.
3. **Voided and re-issued invoices.** A voided invoice's trip line earns no credit. A re-issued one earns exactly one credit. Pinned in Task 10.
4. **A trip across two months** (30 Nov to 1 Dec). A member in Who went counts as having gone in both months. Pinned in Task 10.
5. **Map search down.** If search is unreachable or rate limited (HTTP 429), the picker says so and the address and area fields still save without a pin. Pinned in Tasks 2 and 3.
